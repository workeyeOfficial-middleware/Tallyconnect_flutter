## 4. App — build as usual

## What changes for users

- **Bill details**
  - Bill type now shows "Sales" or "Purchase".
  - Bill date and Credit period appear once bills have been sent again (within a day after the backend is deployed).
- **Outstanding graph:** "By Ageing" and "By Party", with Tally's terms (Not due, Overdue …).
- **Settings → Payment reminders**
  - The admin chooses Automatic or Manual delivery.
  - The admin chooses before the due date, after it, or both, with the number of days, a repeat, and the time.
  - Automatic: the server emails each customer at that hour. It sends to each customer and set of bills at most once a day, using the default template, and only to customers with an email address in Tally.
  - Manual: Email reminder lists the customers due for a reminder today first.
  - The phone's due-date alerts follow the same settings.
- **Reports and lists:** every period bar offers All time, Today, Yesterday, Last 7 days, This month, Next month, Current financial year, Previous financial year and Custom date range. Sales analytics uses the same list, except All time.
- **Graphs:** now also on Cash Flow, Expenses and the official Tally reports. Bar, Column, Line, Scatter and Pie are available.
- **Cards:** the financial statement tiles, voucher type tiles and Receivable / Payable cards can be pinned, hidden, shared and dragged (long-press).
- **Receivable / Payable lists:** tap "Select", tick bills, then share them as one WhatsApp message, one PDF list, or one PDF per bill.
- **AI assistant:** share one answer (with its question) or the whole chat on WhatsApp.
- **Login:** clean layout, with no app icon and no "Welcome back".
