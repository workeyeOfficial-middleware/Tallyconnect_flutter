## 4. App: build as usual

## What users get

### Sales Team page (admin)

**Employee attendance**
- One card per employee showing present days of working days, hours, leave days and absent days.
- Average check-in and check-out times.
- Date choices: Today, Yesterday, Last 7 days, This month, Last month, or Custom (up to 92 days).
- Tap a card for that person's history, a month calendar and their leave.

**Check-in rules**
- Tick the positions that don't check in (e.g. Manager, Director).
- Set each member's position.
- Per person, choose "By position", "Always" or "Exempt".
- Exempt members:
  - aren't asked to check in;
  - can't check in;
  - show as "Not tracked", never "Not checked in" or absent.

### Check-in history (member and admin)

- List or Calendar.
- The calendar colours each day: present (with hours), leave, holiday, weekly off or absent.
- Tap a day to see its details.

### Party list → Location

- Choose a state, or type a city, area or PIN. "Mumbai" matches the address; "400" matches the start of the PIN.
- The list shows only the matching ledgers.
- "Share these" ticks all of them for one WhatsApp message or one PDF. The location is included.
- One ledger still shares from its long-press menu or its screen.
- Normal search also finds ledgers by location.
- Only the address, PIN and state saved in Tally are matched. A ledger without an address in Tally can't match a city.
