Four rules
1
Say it like people talk
“To get”, not “Receivable”. Tally words stay small, in brackets.
2
Picture first, word second
Every feature owns one icon and one colour, everywhere.
3
One big action per screen
56 px buttons, 44 px minimum touch, steps with numbers.
4
Glass never hides text
Blur 26 px + saturate; text is always solid ink, 4.5:1 or better.
